# PlasmaPen Nexus — shared brief for every builder

Goal: a parametric Autodesk Fusion model of the PlasmaPen Nexus (handheld cold-plasma wound pen,
see the pitch summary below), generated by a Fusion Python script. ONE Fusion design contains:
  • "működés" (render) assembly — pen pointing down at a petri dish, plasma plume on, LEDs glowing
  • "engineering" assembly — same pen lying horizontal, shells cut in half (z > 0 removed) so the
    internals are visible. Internal parts are SHARED components (same component, second occurrence),
    shells are built twice (once whole, once cut).
Quality bar: Apple-grade industrial design, real part dimensions, renders that look like a product
page. Plain, restrained, no fake detail. Production-ready code: no TODOs, no stubs.

Pitch facts: 280 g target, 5–8 W, < 38 °C plasma, TDK CeraPlas piezo, 405 nm blue LED (+ 660 nm
aux) excites porphyrin (red) / pyoverdine (green) fluorescence, Omnivision OV5640 camera + filter
in the head, ESP32-S3 (BLE) does closed-loop dosing, one button, 21700 Li-ion 4800 mAh, CNC
aluminium body + PEEK head. Demo: KI-starch strip, BSL-1 agar plate (E. coli K-12 / B. subtilis).
Revision 4 deviations (reasons in "REVISION 4"): CeraPlas HF instead of F (5 W), 1.91" touch
AMOLED instead of the 0.96" OLED, no USB-C (4-pad dock), two galvanically separated boards.

## Rules for builder modules (S, H, E)
- Import only `pp_core` helpers (`neg`, `Op`) and talk only to the `bk` Backend object. NEVER import
  adsk. Never call methods not in the Backend interface. Do not import another builder module.
- Every size/coordinate is a Fusion expression string built from parameter names in pp_params.py.
  Every literal length carries " mm" ("2.5 mm"), angles " deg". Unitless counts are bare.
- If you need a NEW parameter, do NOT edit pp_params.py. Declare it in your module as
      EXTRA_PARAMS = [("name", "expr", "mm", "Rövid magyar komment"), ...]
  (prefix names with your module: shell_*, head_*, ins_*, el_*, scene_*). Expressions may reference
  pp_params names and your own earlier EXTRA_PARAMS only — never another module's. A module check
  is named <prefix>_chk_* and must stay ≥ 0 (the orchestrator and check.py report every chk).
- Profiles are closed loops in the component XY plane (see pp_core docstring). Fully think through
  the geometry: no zero-length segments, no self-intersection, no coincident duplicate vertices,
  no point that changes side of an axis when parameters vary over the check.py variants.
- Revolve profiles about the X axis must lie entirely at y ≥ 0 and touch y = 0 only on purpose.
- Name every component and body in short Hungarian (e.g. "Orrkúp (PEEK)", "Üveggyűrű"), like Apple
  would label parts: plain nouns. Component names listed in the contracts below are fixed
  (check.py matches some of them).
- Looks / materials: use keys from this list (pp_looks.py):
    looks:     alu, alu_dark, peek, pei (amber translucent insert), glass_window (violet-tint
               clear ring), glass_black (cover glass), glass_frost (end cap light guide), lens,
               led405, led660, plasma_core, plasma_halo, spot405, ceramic, pcb, shield, copper,
               gold, steel, battery_wrap, plastic_black, plastic_white, rubber, fan, agar, petri,
               oled_on
    materials: aluminum, peek, pei, glass, plastic, steel, copper, fr4, battery
- Pass pen=False for scene bodies (plume, dish, agar, spot) so they stay out of the mass report.
- Edge breaks: `bk.fillet(comp, name, body, edges, radius)` and
  `bk.chamfer(comp, name, body, edges, distance)` (equal distance). Both take the same selector
  list, chain tangent edges, and are cosmetic: a failure is logged, returns None, never stops the
  build. Selectors (tolerance 0.05 mm, only edges of `body`):
    ("circle", x, r)          arc/circle centred on the X axis at x, radius r
    ("line_y", x, z) / ("line_x", y, z)   straight edge parallel to Y / X through that point
    ("plane_y", y)            every edge lying entirely in the plane y = value
    ("plane_y", y, 1 | -1)    … only on the z > 0 / z < 0 side (an edge on z = 0 is excluded)
    ("op_edges", op)          every edge bounding a face that `op` (an Op you got back earlier,
                              e.g. the cut that made the facet) created, looked up when the
                              fillet/chamfer runs, so later ops are followed
    ("op_edges", op, "outer") … only where those faces meet the rest of the body: the rim of a
                              sliced flat or pocket, without the edges inside the pocket
    ("plane_x", x)            every edge lying entirely in the plane x = value
    ("minus", sel_a, sel_b)   what sel_a matches minus what sel_b matches, e.g. a facet rim
                              without its edge on the joint face
  Keep the Op of every cut you want to break later (`flat = bk.box(..., op="cut", ...)`).
- `bk.count(expr)` → int value of a unitless parameter now, for a row of N features in a loop
  (dock pads). Fusion moves them on a later parameter edit but never adds or removes one.
- Edges the revolve profile can carry (joint chamfers, the Kamm chamfer at the tail) go INTO the
  profile as a 45° segment, not into a chamfer feature: it cannot fail.

## REVISION 4 — architecture (decided)
Overall: 217.7 × Ø36 mm; section 36 wide × 29 tall at the display, 36 × 25.5 at the tail where
both flats exist; ~205 g
(estimate; check.py measures), centre of mass ~110 mm from the tip. Ø36 is the floor, not a style
choice: at Ø34 the OV5640 no longer fits under the collar, the flex tunnel closes and the dock
pads leave the flat (chk_cam_out, chk_tunnel, chk_dock_w go negative). The F element needs Ø37
and makes the pen 236 mm.

Case = 3 parts:
- "Orrkúp (PEEK)" black PEEK 450G, machined. Non-conductive because of TDK's 20 / 30 mm rule:
  the alu starts at body_x0 = 38.4, its inner edge 31.3 mm from the HV end (chk_hv_alu). Slim beak
  (Ø9.5 → Ø16 over 26 mm) with the nozzle, then the big front chamfer: a 61° conical ring face
  (26 → 31 mm, r 8 → 16.95) carrying the violet glass ring with the camera at θ = 0 and 3 × 405 +
  3 × 660 nm LEDs at r 13.5 behind it, then a short collar to the joint. The beak is slim so the
  user sees the spot past it and the camera sees the wound down to the plume edge (chk_cam_view).
- "Ház (alu)" 6063-T6 extrusion: the interior is a constant double-D bore (circle bore_r 16, flats
  at y = bore_top 9.02 and y = −bore_bot −13.23) with two rail grooves at z = ±bore_r. The outside
  is CNC-machined (revolve + two flat cuts + chamfers), bead-blasted, anodised. Open both ends.
- "Hátsó kupak (PC)" frosted optical polycarbonate light guide, RF-transparent: carries the dock
  pads + magnets on its bottom flat, the dock/charger board, the BLE antenna and the status LED
  (the whole cap glows: charging, dose done). Kamm tail: flat back face, 1.6 mm chamfer.
- "Fedőüveg" 0.7 mm aluminosilicate, bonded into the top flat; the AMOLED is laminated under it.

Insert = "Betét (PEI)", injection-moulded PEI (ULTEM 1000 type), natural amber, 1.0 mm walls.
Its outside is the bore section minus 0.25 mm (prismatic, so it slides the full length); two ribs
ride in the rail grooves. The nose cone clips onto the insert's front wall B0 and the HV liner
passes through it, so nose cone + insert + everything inside is ONE cartridge that slides into the
housing from the front; the cap's spigot bayonets into B3 at the back and clamps the housing between
the nose collar and the cap. Then the display drops into the top pocket (FPC plugs into the AI
board through the panel window) and the button snaps in from outside. Compartments:
  P (plasma island, galvanically isolated): HV liner + CeraPlas HF, plasma board under the liner,
     blower. Between B0 and B1. Air enters through the nose shadow gap (nose_gap 0.4) only.
  A (user side): optics chamber in the nose cone, the flex tunnel on top of the P compartment
     (its own sealed channel, PEI floor 0.5 over the 2 mm PEEK liner), the AI bay (B1 → B2),
     the battery bay (B2 → B3, open top and bottom; the cell's Kapton sleeve faces the alu), the
     cap. Battery, dock, display, touch, camera, button and antenna are all A: every conductor a
     user can touch or plug is on the far side of the barrier from the 20 kV.
  Barrier: B1 + the tunnel floor. The bridge board passes through a sealed window in B1; the
  ISO7741 and the transformer straddle B1's plane. Copper shield foil on B1's A face and the
  anodised housing both tie to A ground (Faraday cage for the 82 kHz drive).
The one capability nobody asked for: vision dose-lock. The camera sits 10.5 mm off-axis, so the
660 nm LED spot moves in the image with distance; the AI board computes the standoff and the
bridge's hardware AND only lets the plasma run while the button is held AND 9 mm ≤ standoff ≤
12 mm. That also guarantees TDK's 20 mm between the HV tip and the patient (chk_hv_target), and
the display under the thumb paints a live coverage map of the dose.

## Outer form (Package S implements, the formulas are fixed)
  front  x ∈ [nose_split, x_max]:  t = (x − nose_split) / (x_max − nose_split)
         r = body_r0 + (body_r − body_r0) · (1 − (1 − t)²)          (zero slope at x_max)
  rear   x ∈ [x_max, pen_len]:     u = (x − x_max) / (body_end − x_max)   (u > 1 on the cap)
         r = body_r − (body_r − body_rt) · u²
  Splines through ≥ 8 points of each formula (as revolution 3 did for the ogive). The cap continues
  the rear formula, so the joint at body_end is G1.
  Top cut (one side-view profile, extrude_sym across Z, applied to the housing AND to the cap):
  above the line (tc_x0, outer_r(tc_x0) + 0.8) → spline, horizontal at its end → (tc_x1, top_y)
  → line → (tc_x2, top_y) → spline rising, horizontal at its start → (pen_len + 1, top_y +
  top_rise). Bottom cut: below (bc_x0, −(outer_r(bc_x0) + 0.8)) → spline → (bc_x1, −bot_y) →
  line → (pen_len + 1, −bot_y). Then chamfer ("op_edges", cut, "outer") by top_ch / bot_ch.
  Because the planes cut a body whose radius changes, both flats come out drop-shaped and their
  chamfer reads as a line of varying width — that is the "generative" quality, earned by geometry.

## Package S — shell (pp_shell.py)
