# PlasmaPen Nexus — shared brief for every builder

Goal: a parametric Autodesk Fusion model of the PlasmaPen Nexus (handheld cold-plasma wound pen,
see the pitch summary below), generated by a Fusion Python script. ONE Fusion design contains:
  • "működés" (render) assembly — pen pointing down at a petri dish, plasma plume on, LEDs glowing
  • "engineering" assembly — same pen lying horizontal, shells cut in half (z > 0 removed) so the
    internals are visible. Internal parts are SHARED components (same component, second occurrence),
    shells are built twice (once whole, once cut).
Quality bar: Apple-grade industrial design, real part dimensions, renders that look like a product
page. Plain, restrained, no fake detail. Production-ready code: no TODOs, no stubs.

Pitch facts: 280 g target, 5–8 W, < 38 °C plasma, TDK CeraPlas piezo (12 V in → 15 kV at tip,
~50 kHz; the model uses the bare CeraPlas F element 72 × 6 × 2.8 so the pen can be Ø25), 405 nm blue LED (+ 660 nm aux) excites porphyrin (red) / pyoverdine (green) fluorescence,
Omnivision OV5640 camera + filter in the head, ESP32-S3 (BLE) does closed-loop PWM dosing,
0.96" OLED, one button, USB-C, 21700 Li-ion 4800 mAh, CNC aluminium body + PEEK head, 4-layer PCB +
BMS. Demo: KI-starch strip, BSL-1 agar plate (E. coli K-12 / B. subtilis).

## Rules for builder modules (A–D)
- Import only `pp_core` helpers (`neg`, `Op`) and talk only to the `bk` Backend object. NEVER import
  adsk. Never call methods not in the Backend interface.
- Every size/coordinate is a Fusion expression string built from parameter names in pp_params.py.
  Every literal length carries " mm" ("2.5 mm"), angles " deg". Unitless counts are bare.
- If you need a NEW parameter, do NOT edit pp_params.py. Declare it in your module as
      EXTRA_PARAMS = [("name", "expr", "mm", "Rövid magyar komment"), ...]
  (prefix names with your area: head_*, body_*, int_*, scene_*). Expressions may reference
  pp_params names and your own earlier EXTRA_PARAMS. The orchestrator adds them after PARAMS.
- Profiles are closed loops in the component XY plane (see pp_core docstring). Fully think through
  the geometry: no zero-length segments, no self-intersection, no coincident duplicate vertices,
  no point that changes side of an axis when parameters vary in a sane ±20 % range.
- Revolve profiles about the X axis must lie entirely at y ≥ 0 and touch y = 0 only on purpose.
- Name every component and body in short Hungarian (e.g. "Fej (PEEK)", "Üveggyűrű"), like Apple
  would label parts: plain nouns.
- Looks / materials: use keys from this list (agent D defines them in pp_looks.py):
    looks:     alu, alu_dark, peek, glass_window (violet-tint clear ring), glass_black (OLED crystal),
               lens, led405, led660, plasma_core, plasma_halo, spot405, ceramic, pcb, shield,
               copper, gold, steel, battery_wrap, plastic_black, plastic_white, rubber, fan,
               agar, petri, oled_on
    materials: aluminum, peek, glass, plastic, steel, copper, fr4, battery
- Pass pen=False for scene bodies (plume, dish, agar, spot) so they stay out of the mass report.

## Module contracts
  pp_head.py:      build_shell(bk, parent, cutaway) -> list[comp]   (PEEK head; cut if cutaway)
                   build_shared(bk, parent) -> list[comp]            (window ring, LEDs, camera)
  pp_body.py:      build_shell(bk, parent, cutaway) -> list[comp]   (alu tube, end cap)
                   build_shared(bk, parent) -> list[comp]            (OLED crystal, button cap)
  pp_internals.py: build_shared(bk, parent) -> list[comp]
  pp_scene.py:     build_scene(bk, parent) -> list[comp]             (render assembly only)
  When cutaway is True, after building, call bk.cut_half(comp, "Metszet", [bodies...]).

## Design direction — REVISION 3 (Patrik: "Apple design, eloxált alu unibody, nagyobb kijelző, lapos oldal, Pininfarina tollak")
One-piece anodised aluminium unibody, Ø29, like a Pininfarina Segno / Forever pen scaled up: a single
body in anodised aluminium, a long slender conical front that flows into the cylinder, ONE flat facet
running the full length on top (+Y) that naturally fades out on the taper (it also works as the
roll-stop), the display and the button sit on that facet. Small black PEEK nose at the tip with the
violet glass ring. The unibody is open at the BACK only: everything (PEEK nose+liner, piezo, fan, boards,
cell) slides in from the back — the taper narrows toward the front, so nothing can enter from the
front. A short aluminium end cap carrying the USB-C closes it (the facet continues across the cap).
The nose is the front end of ONE PEEK part "Orr + bélés (PEEK)" that fills the taper interior and
pokes out through the unibody's front opening as the black tip (small step silver → black, like a
pencil tip). Plain, restrained, Apple-grade. No seams except the nose.

## Frame & layout (mm, default parameters; pp_params.py is the truth — always ExprEval it)
X = pen axis, x = 0 nozzle face, +X toward the back. Y = up (facet, θ = 0). Z = side.
  x 0 … 14       PEEK nose insert ("Orr (PEEK)"), outer r tip_r (10): face with collar r 2.5…4,
                 glass ring r 4…9 (win_t), rim r 9…10; camera at θ = 0 on ring_r 6.6, LEDs around.
                 Nozzle bore r 2.5 x 0 … nozzle_len (14). The nose is a spigot into the unibody.
  x 14 … 76      unibody taper (cone_len 62): outer r 10 → 14.5 (body_r), a smooth curve, tangent
                 to the cylinder at taper_x1 (76) — not a straight cone with a kink. Alu wall in the
                 taper = front_wall (1.5).
  x 76 … 233.05  unibody cylinder r 14.5, bore r 11.1 (bore_r), open at the back (body_end).
  x 233.05 … 238.05  alu end cap ("Hátsó kupak (alu)") cap_len + cap_spigot into the bore, USB-C
                 stadium opening, back outer edge round back_r, facet cut continues over it.
  facet          everything above y = flat_y (12.2) is cut away along the whole length → 15.67 mm
                 wide flat on the cylinder, fading out on the taper where r < 12.2.
  x 14 … 91      PEEK liner ("Bélés (PEEK)") filling the unibody inside: outer = alu inner surface
                 (taper inner surface, then r 11.1), inner bore r liner_ri (7) around the piezo,
                 funnel from the nozzle bore to r 7 at funnel_x1 (20). Wiring channel at θ = 0.

                 mounts at ¼ and ¾ inside the liner bore r 7.

                 under the glass); tact switch under the button
  x 111          button Ø7 on the facet, proud by btn_proud
  x 118.5 … 151  display glass (flush in the facet, glass_t 0.55), size oled_win_len × oled_win_w
                 (32.54 × 13.56), corner radius ~1.5
  x 119.3 … 150.2  1.1" AMOLED 126×294 panel 30.94 × 12.96 × 1.3 at y 10.35 … 11.65 in a pocket
                 milled into the unibody under the glass (pocket opens into the bore for the FPC);
                 active area 25.578 × 10.962 centred → body "OLED aktív terület"
  driver PCB     top face y -4, width 16, x 105 … 153
  x 157 … 227.3  21700 cell Ø21.4 in bore r 11.1
  x 228.8 … 230.4  BMS/USB board Ø20; USB-C receptacle behind it through the back wall, 0.3 recess
  x < 0          plume, dish
